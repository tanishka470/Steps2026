Camera-Ready Submission

Authors are requested to prepare and upload the revised/camera-ready version of the paper through the Microsoft CMT portal.

  1. Carefully address and incorporate the reviewers' comments and recommendations.
  2. Follow the prescribed conference formatting (https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates) and submission guidelines.
  3. Plagiarism in the camera-ready paper should be below 20% (excluding references) and below 30% (including references).
  4. The AI content of the camera-ready paper should be zero %.
  5. The camera-ready paper should be uploaded through the CMT portal only after having passed through IEEE PDF Express (https://ieee-pdf-express.org/Account/Login).

PDF Instructions for Authors

a.   Before Creating a PDF
Proofread your source document thoroughly before creating a PDF. No revisions should be needed after this point.

b.   Creating Your PDF eXpress Account

  1. Log in to the IEEE PDF eXpressTM site.

First-time users:

  1. Create an account.
  2. Enter: 71724X for Conference ID, your email address, and a password.
  3. Continue as prompted. A confirmation will be displayed and emailed to you.

Previous users: follow the same steps using your existing password. Verify your contact information is current.

c.   Uploading and Checking Files

Dashboard: Click Create New Title after logging in to start a new paper submission.
Upload: Enter your paper title, then choose to upload either a PDF for checking or source files (like Word or compressed TeX archives) for conversion.
Review: Wait for the check or conversion process to complete.

d.   Approving and Submitting

Download: If successful, download your verified, IEEE Xplore-compatible PDF.
Approve: Click the Approve button to submit the certified PDF directly to your conference editor.

Please note that the final publication of the paper is subject to compliance with the conference's publication and submission requirements.

IEEE Copyright Form

Authors are also required to complete and submit the IEEE Copyright Form through the Microsoft CMT portal as part of the camera-ready submission process.

Please ensure that the copyright form is completed correctly and submitted through the designated CMT interface within the specified deadline. The paper cannot be processed for publication without completion of the required copyright formalities.

STEPS 2026

The International Conference on Sustainable Technologies for Energy and Power Systems (STEPS 2026) is an exceptional gathering of minds dedicated to advancing the field of power and energy systems. With a diverse program featuring keynote speeches, technical paper presentations, panel discussions, and workshops, STEPS 2026 covers a wide spectrum of topics ranging from renewable energy integration to cyber security in energy systems.

Prof. (Dr.) Bhim Singh

ANRF National Science Chair & Emeritus Professor, Department of Electrical Engineering, IIT Delhi

Photo Coming Soon

Abstract / Overview

Prof. Bhim Singh is one of India’s most distinguished power and energy researchers, known for decades of leadership in renewable energy systems, power quality, electric drives, and advanced converters. His work spans academic research, national missions, policy initiatives, and conference leadership, with significant contributions to education, innovation, and technology translation.

Education & Career

  • B.E. (Electrical), University of Roorkee, 1977.
  • M.Tech. (Power Apparatus & Systems), IIT Delhi, 1979.
  • Ph.D., IIT Delhi, 1984.
  • Joined University of Roorkee (now IIT Roorkee) in 1983 as Lecturer; became Reader in 1988.
  • Joined IIT Delhi in December 1990; became Associate Professor (1994) and Professor (1997).
  • Served as ABB Chair Professor (2007–2012), CEA Chair Professor (2012–2017 and 2019–2021), Head of Department (2014–2016), and Dean Academics (2016–2019) at IIT Delhi.
  • JC Bose Fellow (DST, Government of India), December 2015–June 2021.
  • Currently ANRF National Science Chair and Emeritus Professor at IIT Delhi (since July 2021).
  • Appointed Prime Minister Professor at NSUT (from July 2026), while continuing as Honorary Professor at IIT Delhi.

Current Roles & Leadership

  • Chairperson, Board of Governors (BOG), SVNIT Surat (from 25 August 2023, three-year term).
  • Former Chairman, BOG, MANIT Bhopal (from 3 July 2018, three-year term).
  • Former Non-official Independent Director, NTPC Limited (from 17 July 2018, three-year term).
  • Chairman, All India GATE 2006; Organizing Chairman, All India JAM 2006.
  • Chairman of Indian Delegation on Smart Grid for Indo-UK Workshop (June 2012).
  • Expert Member, PAC of SERB-DST India (since 2012); Steering Committee Member for NAMPET phase II and III.

Research, Supervision & Innovation

  • Guided 162 Ph.D. dissertations and 184 M.E./M.Tech./M.S.(R) theses.
  • Filed 109 patents.
  • Executed more than 90 sponsored and consultancy projects.
  • Co-authored the textbook Power Quality: Power Quality Problems and Mitigation Techniques (John Wiley & Sons, 2015).

Research Interests

  • Solar photovoltaic and wind energy generation
  • Solar water pumping and renewable energy charging stations
  • Microgrids and energy-efficient appliances
  • Power quality and improved quality converters
  • Multi-pulse converters, electric vehicles, power electronics
  • Electric machines, electric drives, and CAD of electrical machines
  • FACTS and HVDC systems

Fellowships & Memberships

Prof. Singh is a Fellow of FNAE, INSA (FNA), NASI (FNASc), IASc (FASc), TWAS (FTWAS), IEEE (FIEEE), IET (FIET), IE(I) (FIE), and IETE (FIETE). He is also a Life Member of ISTE, SSI, and NIQR.

Selected Awards & Honors

  • Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar – Vigyan Shri (2024)
  • IIT Roorkee Distinguished Alumnus Award (2023)
  • Goyal Prize in Applied Sciences (2021–2022)
  • International Kalpana Chawla Solar Award (2020)
  • INAE Outstanding Teacher Award (2020)
  • IEEE IAS Outstanding Educator/Mentor Award (2020)
  • Eminent Engineer Award, IE(I) Delhi State Centre (2020)
  • IIT Delhi Faculty Life Time Achievement Award (2018)
  • IEEE PES Nari Hingorani Custom Power Award (2017, USA)
  • Shri Om Prakash Bhasin Award in Engineering including Energy & Aerospace (2014)

Conference & Professional Service

  • General Chair/Co-Chair for major IEEE conferences including PEDES 2006 and 2010, and key roles in IEEE-INDICON 2015, IEEE-ICPS 2016, IEEE-ICPECA 2019, and others.
  • Former Chair, IEEE PES-IAS Delhi Chapter (2005–2010), PELS-IES Delhi Chapter (Founder Chair, 2007–2010), and IEEE Delhi Section (2012–2014).
  • IEEE PES Distinguished Lecturer for a decade from 2005.
  • Chaired 100+ conference sessions and delivered 300+ invited talks, keynotes, and memorial lectures.
  • Faculty Mentor to multiple successful start-ups, including Quanteon, HyperX Energy, Intellicon Technologies, Tadpole Projects, and Tsuyo Manufacturing.

STEPS 2026

The International Conference on Sustainable Technologies for Energy and Power Systems (STEPS 2026) is an exceptional gathering of minds dedicated to advancing the field of power and energy systems. With a diverse program featuring keynote speeches, technical paper presentations, panel discussions, and workshops, STEPS 2026 covers a wide spectrum of topics ranging from renewable energy integration to cyber security in energy systems.
